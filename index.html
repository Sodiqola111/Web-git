Description

Lordicon offers a unique library of modern, animated icons designed
to bring life and interactivity to your WordPress site. With this plugin,
you can seamlessly browse, customize, and insert Lordicon icons
directly into the Gutenberg editor.

Features

  • Extensive free icon set (with attribution)
  • Full icon library available in PRO version (no attribution)
  • Insert icons directly into Gutenberg blocks
  • Customize color, stroke, animation type, and trigger

Why Choose Lordicon?

Fully Customizable

Modify icons directly within WordPress: choose colors, animation triggers (hover, click, loop), and more.
